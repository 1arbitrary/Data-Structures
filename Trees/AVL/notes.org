* AVL Rotation Notes

** P.S. while thinking about rotations

Honestly, the thing that finally made rotations click for me was stopping thinking in terms of “moving pointers around” and instead thinking about BST ordering.

For a right rotation:
- the new root comes from the left subtree, so it HAS to be smaller than the old root.
- because of that, the old root naturally becomes the right child of the new root.

The other important observation:
- the old right child of the new root (T2) cannot disappear.
- it is greater than the new root but still smaller than the old root, so it fits perfectly as the left child/subtree of the old root after rotation.

Same logic applies symmetrically for left rotation. Make sure to keep the heights of nodes after relinking nodes.

This made rotations feel much less like memorizing cases and much more like preserving inorder ordering.
